# Exam Marathon (シカクマラソン) Support

[日本語](support) | [English](support-en)

## App Overview

Exam Marathon helps you keep studying for certification exams every day. A runner runs toward your exam date as the goal. When you record your study, the runner's condition improves. You can also use "phone stop", which blocks the apps you choose for the time you choose.

## Supported Platforms

- iOS 16 or later

## Main Features

- Registering exams (choose from the name suggestions or type your own), with the exam date shown as the goal
- Daily study records and rest days
- A runner whose condition changes with your records
- Phone stop (blocks the apps you choose for the time you choose)
- A notification at the time you set
- Recording a pass date, and a pass medal
- Light and dark appearance

## Free Version and Pro

- Free version: Ads are shown. You can register one exam in total
- Pro (one-time purchase): No ads. You can register your next exam even after an exam is over. You can register up to 3 exams at the same time whose exam dates have not passed

To use your Pro purchase on another device, or after reinstalling the app, tap "Restore purchase" on the Pro purchase screen.
