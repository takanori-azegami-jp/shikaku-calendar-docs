# Exam Marathon (シカクマラソン) Privacy Policy

[日本語](index) | [English](index-en)

## Privacy Policy for the iOS Application

This Privacy Policy explains how Takanori Azegami ("the developer") handles user information in the iOS application "Exam Marathon" (Japanese name: シカクマラソン) ("the app").

### 1. Basic Policy

The developer recognizes the importance of user information and will handle information used by the app appropriately while respecting applicable laws, regulations, and guidelines.

### 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies only to this app.

### 3. Overview of the App

The app helps you keep studying for certification exams every day. A runner runs toward your exam date as the goal. When you record your study, the runner's condition improves. You can also use "phone stop", which blocks the apps you choose for the time you choose.

#### 3.1 Features and Permissions Used

The app uses the following features and permissions. Each one is requested on a system screen.

- Notifications: Used to send a study reminder at the time you set
- Screen Time (Family Controls): Used by "phone stop" to block the apps you choose for the time you choose
- Tracking (App Tracking Transparency): Used to ask whether the identifier used for advertising may be used

If you decline any of these, the rest of the app works as usual.

### 4. Information Collected and Stored

#### 4.1 Information Stored on the Device

The app stores the following information only on your device:

- The names, difficulty levels, and exam dates of the exams you register
- Your study records, rest days, and pass dates
- Settings such as the notification time and appearance
- The state of "phone stop" (such as the end time) and your selection of apps

The app does not send this information to the developer's servers.

#### 4.2 Information Handled by "Phone Stop"

For the apps blocked by "phone stop", the app does not read how long or how often they are used. It does not store or send such information. Your selection of apps is stored only on your device.

#### 4.3 Personal Information

The developer does not automatically collect personally identifiable information such as names, addresses, or email addresses through the app. There is no account registration.

Only when you send an email from the app's request and inquiry screen, the developer receives your email address, the content of the email, the app version, and the OS version. This information is used to reply, to investigate bugs, and to consider improvements to the app.

### 5. Third-Party Services

#### 5.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

The free version displays ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. The app shows a full-screen ad (interstitial ad) at most once a day. If you purchase Pro, no ads are shown.

To display ads, Google may collect the following information:

- Approximate location
- Device ID (such as the advertising identifier)
- Product interaction (information about ad impressions and interactions)
- Advertising data
- Crash data and performance data (used for app functionality)

Except for crash data and performance data, this information is used for third-party advertising and may be used for tracking. Google handles this information in accordance with Google's privacy policy.

- If you allow tracking: the advertising identifier is used to deliver ads. Ads tailored to you may be shown
- If you do not allow tracking: the advertising identifier is not used. Ads are still shown. The app's features do not change

You can change the tracking permission at any time in your device's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. For more information, please refer to:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

#### 5.2 Apple (In-App Purchase)

Pro is purchased as a one-time in-app purchase on the App Store. Apple processes the payment. The developer does not receive payment information such as credit card numbers. The purchase state is checked through communication with the App Store.

### 6. Purposes of Use

Information handled by the app is used for the following purposes:

- Displaying your study records and calculating the runner's condition
- Sending notifications at the time you set
- Blocking the apps you choose for the time you choose
- Displaying ads in the free version
- Checking the purchase state of Pro
- Responding to emails you send to us

### 7. Opening External Pages and the Email App

The app may open the App Store review screen and the page of this Privacy Policy. The app shows a confirmation before opening them. It does not attach your usage, registered exams, records, or device-identifying information when opening them.

The request and inquiry screen opens your email app with the recipient, subject, and message filled in. The app version and the OS version are added at the end of the message. Your registered exams, records, and usage are not added. You send the email in your email app.

The app may also show the standard iOS review prompt. iOS decides how often it is shown.

### 8. User Control and Deletion

- You can delete an exam from the exam list. The records, pass date, and runner of that exam are deleted with it. This cannot be undone
- You can delete all of the app's data on your device by uninstalling the app

### 9. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

### 10. Security

The developer strives to manage app data appropriately under a local-storage-based design. However, the developer does not guarantee the security of the user's device, operating system, or external services.

### 11. Disclaimer

The developer is not responsible for damages arising from the use of the app except where required by law. The app does not guarantee that you will pass an exam.
